# Controls

Star Forge is built exclusively for touchscreen play on Android. All gameplay input is handled through on-screen touch controls. There are no keyboard bindings, mouse controls, cursor interactions, or desktop gameplay modes.

## Flight

- Touch the lower-left flight zone to summon a floating joystick under your thumb.
- Drag in the direction you want the ship to face; farther movement applies more thrust.
- Release the joystick to stop steering and thrusting. The ship coasts with its existing momentum.
- Hold **BOOST** for increased acceleration and speed.
- Hold **FIRE** to shoot while sufficient weapon energy is available.

## World interaction

- Tap **LOOT** to collect the nearest eligible salvage crate.
- Tap **FTL** to open the galaxy map. Select a directly connected system and tap **JUMP**. Each jump consumes 5 Fuel.
- Tap **BAG** to open the cargo panel.
- Tap **SAVE** to save the current game.

## Cargo panel

- Select a commodity using its item button.
- Use **- QTY** and **+ QTY** to change the amount selected for jettison.
- Tap **JETTISON** to drop the selected quantity as a physical salvage crate.
- Tap **SELL AT STATION** to sell the entire selected commodity stack while within station range.
- Toggle **NPC LOOT ON/OFF** to allow or prevent nearby NPCs from collecting salvage.
- Tap **CLOSE** to close the panel.

## Display and input

The game launches in landscape orientation and scales its touch interface with the available Android display. Touch controls are the only supported gameplay input method.
